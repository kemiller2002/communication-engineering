# Evidence Record

## Evidence summary

ISO 9241-11 defines usability as an outcome of specified users achieving specified goals with effectiveness, efficiency, and satisfaction in a specified context of use.

## Exact claim supported or contradicted

Supports using task success, effort/time, and satisfaction as complementary measures for a bounded communication intervention.

## Source provenance

Published international standard, edition 2, confirmed under ISO's review lifecycle. The public abstract and terminology page were inspected.

## Relevant excerpt or data

The standard's definition binds users, goals, outcomes, and context rather than treating usability as a fixed artifact attribute.

## Interpretation

A procedural-documentation slice can be evaluated with observable, falsifiable outcomes and can separate effectiveness from efficiency and preference.

## Limitations

The full standard is paywalled; this record relies on ISO's official abstract and public terminology. The standard defines concepts but does not prescribe a specific study method.

## Counterevidence

Communication can also seek trust, deliberation, learning, identity, or long-term attitude change; task measures do not exhaust those purposes.
