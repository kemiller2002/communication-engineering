# Evidence Record

## Summary and claim

The selected writers span English/Igbo relations, Spanish, Arabic, Bengali,
Chinese, and Palestinian Arabic contexts. English editions can support
high-level comparisons of narrative architecture and themes, but claims about
syntax, rhythm, diction, register, names, or cultural semantics depend on
translator and edition.

## Provenance and limitations

Reference bibliographies and publisher/author records were checked for major
works. This record derives a methodological boundary; it is not reception
evidence and does not compare original-language passages.

## Counterevidence

Achebe wrote primarily in English and Tagore translated/reworked some of his
own work, but even these cases involve multilingual literary systems and should
not be treated as translation-free.
