# Evidence Record

## Evidence summary

The article identifies covering-law, inference-to-best-explanation, and
semantic interpretations of *Origin*, argues that each has textual support but
fails as a complete account, and proposes three causal-efficacy strategies.

## Exact claim supported or contradicted

Supports caution against assigning one simple global progression to a complex
work and provides a competing interpretation to the pilot's chapter-level code.

## Source provenance

Peer-reviewed *Philosophy of Science* article; DOI metadata and abstract were
reviewed. Full argument was not independently reproduced from raw annotations.

## Relevant excerpt or data

The abstract explicitly states that multiple models have textual support and
that none captures the overall strategy before proposing an alternative.

## Interpretation

Rhetorical coding is theory-laden. The pilot must remain unit-specific and
record alternative readings instead of treating code labels as observations.

## Limitations

This evidence concerns Darwin and philosophy-of-science interpretation, not
reader comprehension. Access in this cycle was limited to publisher metadata
and abstract.

## Counterevidence

disagree, provided the unit and evidence are explicit.
