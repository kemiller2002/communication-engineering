# Evidence Record

## Evidence summary

“Mending Wall” embeds a repeated maxim within competing voices and shared work,
preventing the famous line from functioning as an unqualified authorial rule.
“After Apple-Picking” moves from sensory labor into uncertain dream and sleep.

## Claim and interpretation

Supports dramatic countervoice and concrete-to-ambiguous transition as
deliberative or experiential patterns.

## Provenance and limits

Public-domain texts hosted by Poetry Foundation. Interpretation is constrained
by the dramatic situation but not uniquely settled; performance and meter are
not fully captured by prose coding.

## Counterevidence

Readers routinely extract slogans from Frost, showing that ambiguity does not
guarantee context-sensitive reception.
