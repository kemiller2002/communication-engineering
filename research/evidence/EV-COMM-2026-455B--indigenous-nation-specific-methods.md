# Evidence Record

## Summary and claim

Momaday (Kiowa), Silko (Laguna Pueblo), Harjo (Mvskoke), Ortiz (Acoma Pueblo),
and Long Soldier (Oglala Lakota) use different relations among land, nation,
oral/written form, colonial documents, family, return, ceremony, and language.
The comparison supports nation-specific analysis and rejects a universal
“Indigenous storytelling” style.

## Provenance and limitations

Author records, interviews, hosted poems, and Indigenous-literature collections
were reviewed. This desk study does not confer community authority, determine
permissions, or analyze original Indigenous languages.

## Counterevidence

Colonial schooling, land dispossession, sovereignty, oral traditions, and
survival create meaningful comparative questions, provided comparison does not
erase nation and protocol.
