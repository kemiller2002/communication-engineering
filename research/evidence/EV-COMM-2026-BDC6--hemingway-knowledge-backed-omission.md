# Evidence Record

## Evidence summary

The novel repeatedly places emotional and bodily stakes beneath concrete
dialogue, travel, ritual, and behavior. Hemingway's later iceberg formulation
distinguishes informed omission from omission caused by ignorance.

## Claim and interpretation

Supports “knowledge-backed omission”: implicitness can concentrate inference
only when underlying structure is known and recoverable.

## Provenance and limits

Project Gutenberg 1926 text plus widely documented author commentary from
*Death in the Afternoon*. This cycle did not inspect a stable primary scan of
the latter, so the theoretical link is Medium confidence. Cultural prerequisites
and gendered representation limit transfer.

## Counterevidence

Many passages are descriptive or explicit; “short sentences” do not explain
the novel's larger sequencing or subtext.
