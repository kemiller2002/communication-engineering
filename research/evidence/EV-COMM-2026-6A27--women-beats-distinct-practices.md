# Evidence Record

## Summary and claim

Di Prima joins stream-of-consciousness, form, politics, spirituality, and
motherhood; Kyger's journals and poems privilege daily observation; weiss's
work is bound to jazz performance, typography, exile, and memory. This supports
separate functional profiles rather than a male-centered spontaneous-prose
default.

## Provenance and limitations

Author biographies, bibliographies, hosted works, and publisher/archive context
were reviewed. Scholarship and stable digital primary access were uneven for
Kyger and weiss, so claims are Medium confidence and require archival follow-up.

## Counterevidence

All participated in overlapping Beat/San Francisco networks and shared interests
in performance, Buddhism, and open form; distinctness does not imply isolation.
