# Evidence Record

## Evidence summary

A systematic review found that guidelines for plain-language summaries contain many criteria, but direct empirical support was absent for most of them.

## Exact claim supported or contradicted

Contradicts treating popular plain-language rules as a validated, complete theory of effective communication.

## Source provenance

Peer-reviewed systematic review integrating theory, guidelines, and empirical studies. Full text was available through PubMed Central.

## Relevant excerpt or data

The authors explicitly report that they did not find empirical evidence supporting most identified criteria.

## Interpretation

The project must test bundles and mechanisms, preserve uncertainty, and avoid converting style guidance into universal laws.

## Limitations

The review concerns plain-language summaries of research, not all procedural or persuasive communication. Absence of evidence is not evidence of no effect.

## Counterevidence

Individual controlled studies do demonstrate benefits for specific plain-language formats and outcomes.
