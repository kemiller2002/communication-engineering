# Evidence Record

## Evidence summary

The thesis finds that Asimov adapts popular-science writing through direct
address, rhetorical questions, autobiographical comment, wordplay, and an
informal personal voice. *Foundation* and “The Relativity of Wrong” were used as
bounded comparative cases for idea-first modeling.

## Claim and interpretation

Supports audience-aware conceptual scaffolding, not a claim that all Asimov
writing is simple or effective.

## Provenance and limits

University of Tennessee master's thesis; independent but dated. Full primary
texts for copyrighted Asimov works were not reproduced. Asimov's immense output
and changing genres prohibit a single-style conclusion.

## Counterevidence

Idea-centered dialogue may weaken characterization, obscure social contingency,
or overload audiences lacking the conceptual prerequisites.
