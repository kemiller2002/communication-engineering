# Hypothesis

For a procedural document whose purpose is immediate task execution,
unassisted correct completion is the primary outcome. Task-invalidating or
critical errors and assistance are guardrails; time, workload, satisfaction,
and confidence are secondary diagnostics. A delayed no-document repeat or
novel transfer task is required only for durable-learning claims.

# Falsification criteria

Reject or revise this vector if task analysis cannot produce reliable goal
states and error codes, if independent raters cannot score it consistently, if
the instrument has floor/ceiling effects, or if representative users show that
the selected task purpose is not immediate execution.

# Evidence assessment

Standards and official human-factors guidance support multidimensional,
context-bound outcomes and separate unassisted completion, assistance, time,
and errors [EV-COMM-2026-0002, EV-COMM-2026-0007, EV-COMM-2026-0008].
Observed weak subjective/objective agreement makes satisfaction unsafe as a
primary proxy [EV-COMM-2026-0009]. Learning research narrows the retention
requirement to claims of durable learning [EV-COMM-2026-0010].

# Unknowns

Task-specific scoring reliability, minimum detectable difference, participant
burden, accessibility accommodations, acceptable floor/ceiling thresholds, and
whether binary or partial-credit completion is more sensitive.

# Implications

The measurement architecture is ready for task-specific feasibility design,
but not for an effectiveness claim or a universal composite score.
