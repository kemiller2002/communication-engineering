# Hypothesis

## Statement

For mixed-expertise technical audiences, a short linear conceptual overview
linked to a canonical non-linear reference layer will outperform either layer
alone on combined orientation and retrieval outcomes.

## Mechanism

The overview builds a global schema; the reference graph supports precise
lookup without forcing experts through tutorial prose.

## Predictions

Novices will orient faster than with reference-only material; experts will
retrieve exact requirements faster than with overview-only material; drift
between layers will erase the advantage.

## Evidence that would support it

Randomized task study measuring initial mental-model accuracy, delayed
retrieval, critical interpretation errors, and assistance by expertise.

## Evidence that would contradict it

No improvement over a well-designed single layer, or increased errors caused
by conflicting abstraction levels.

## Tests performed

Desk application to technical instruction and research-handoff cases only.

## Results

Untested outcome hypothesis; architecture is plausible but not validated.

## Falsification attempts

Identified link chasing, maintenance drift, and duplicated definitions as
credible mechanisms of harm.

## Current assessment

Proposed at Medium (0.52 judgment).

## Next experiment

Compare overview-only, reference-only, and linked two-layer documentation on a
versioned API task.
