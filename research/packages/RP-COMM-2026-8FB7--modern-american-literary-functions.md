# Executive Summary

The comparison adds seven bounded literary functions to Communication
Engineering: Fitzgerald's symbolic convergence, Hemingway's knowledge-backed
omission, Frost's dramatic countervoice, Asimov's conceptual crisis-and-
reversal, Ginsberg's anaphoric witness, Kerouac's generative momentum, and
Ferlinghetti's mobile civic observer [EV-COMM-2026-3DFC through
EV-COMM-2026-2676].

The central result is an inference-contract hypothesis: literary transfer works
when a target context can safely preserve what readers must infer, tolerate as
ambiguous, feel, remember, and verify [HY-COMM-2026-45A8]. Hemingway-style
omission is therefore unsuitable for safety or evidence chains; Frost-like
irresolution fits deliberation but not emergency action; Ginsbergian escalation
fits ethical witness but not neutral synthesis; Asimovian simplification needs
explicit contingency and human consequences. Confidence is Medium because the
textual distinctions are strong but causal effects are untested.

# Current Understanding

Literary techniques allocate interpretive labor. Motif asks readers to retain
and recombine; omission asks them to infer; dramatic voice asks them to hold
conflict; conceptual fiction asks them to model; catalogue asks them to absorb
accumulation; spontaneity privileges discovery; a mobile observer privileges
selection and motion. Transfer succeeds only if that labor fits purpose, risk,
expertise, power, and accessibility.

# Key Discoveries

1. Hemingway's useful abstraction is informed, recoverable omission—not short
   sentences [EV-COMM-2026-BDC6].
2. Frost's famous maxim is a countervoice inside a dramatic conflict, warning
   against quote-level extraction [EV-COMM-2026-3FFC].
3. Fitzgerald and Hemingway both rely on inference but on different memory
   horizons: structural recurrence versus scene-level subtext [EV-3DFC;
   EV-BDC6].
4. Asimov's idea-first strength and character/social-model weakness are linked:
   both follow abstraction priority [EV-COMM-2026-E90A].
5. Ginsberg, Kerouac, and Ferlinghetti should not be collapsed into one Beat
   style [EV-COMM-2026-2676].

# Failed Assumptions

- “Beat” denotes one method: rejected.
- Spare prose is inherently clear: rejected; implicit context can increase load.
- Quotable lines state a poem's thesis: rejected by Frost's dramatic context.
- Spontaneous means uncrafted or publication-ready: rejected.
- Idea-centered fiction is socially neutral: rejected; model selection embeds
  power and omission.
- Literary greatness proves practical transfer: rejected.
