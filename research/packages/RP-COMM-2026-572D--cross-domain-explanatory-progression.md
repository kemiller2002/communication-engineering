# Executive Summary

A five-domain pilot supports a conditional model: purpose, audience
prerequisites, use mode, authority, and proof obligation explain more of a
text's progression architecture than a whole-writer style label. Darwin builds
a difficult causal mechanism through familiar analogy, heterogeneous cases,
objections, and synthesis. Euclid exposes a short dependency chain under shared
primitives. *Brown* narrows an institutional question, acknowledges historical
limits, connects present function and effect, and states a holding. Descartes
makes a problem experiential before compressing a method into four rules. RFC
9110 layers scope, terms, abstractions, normative rules, and navigation for
expert retrieval [EV-COMM-2026-D6B2; EV-COMM-2026-6A2D;
EV-COMM-2026-8434; EV-COMM-2026-5479; EV-COMM-2026-3377].

The result supports `TH-COMM-2026-0001` but does not establish causal audience
effects. Confidence is Medium (0.63 judgment): primary-text order is directly
observable and repeated coding was 12/15, while the tiny, Western, English-
readable corpus confounds author, genre, era, and domain. The largest caveat is
that fit recommendations remain hypotheses until audience experiments compare
architectures on purpose-bound outcomes.

# Current Understanding

Explanatory progression is best modeled as a sequence of functions that moves
an audience from a declared or inferred starting representation to a target
representation. Every selected text orients, manages prerequisites, connects
support to a claim under a domain-specific warrant, and closes. What changes is
the order, explicitness, and acceptable omission:

- closed proof systems foreground dependencies;
- historical causal explanations accumulate and reconcile evidence;
- judicial opinions balance legal warrant, institutional legitimacy, and
  closure;
- reflective philosophy can motivate abstraction through narrated inquiry;
- specifications trade linear ease for stable definitions and retrieval.

These are bounded patterns, not author essences. A useful recommendation has
the form: for purpose P, audience A, use mode M, proof/risk regime R, use
functional pattern T, expect mechanism hypothesis X, avoid failure F, and test
outcome O.

# Key Discoveries

1. **Warrant and example are not the same move.** Darwin's examples can explain
   and support without having Euclid's deductive force; the rubric was revised
   to preserve this distinction [EV-COMM-2026-D6B2; EV-COMM-2026-6A2D].
2. **Navigation is part of architecture.** RFC 9110's section graph supports
   recurrent expert retrieval, making linear readability an incomplete quality
   criterion [EV-COMM-2026-3377; HY-COMM-2026-CFF5].
3. **Authority is contextual, not stylistic.** Brown's institutional voice and
   unanimity cannot be responsibly copied by a communicator without comparable
   authority and process [EV-COMM-2026-8434].
4. **Compression requires practice support.** Descartes's four rules are
   memorable, but scholarship treats them as a practice-dependent abridgment
   rather than a complete universal procedure [EV-COMM-2026-5479;
   EV-COMM-2026-7D8D].
5. **Whole-work interpretations can conflict.** Multiple scholarly models of
   Darwin's “long argument” have textual support, so coding must remain
   unit-bound and preserve alternatives [EV-COMM-2026-6F00].
6. **High-risk protocols can dominate writer choice.** Medical summaries, UI
   warnings, and sensitive interpersonal messages require safety, consent,
   action, and relational constraints before writer-derived architecture.

## Provisional concept and glossary records

No canonical concept/glossary registry or schema exists. To avoid inventing a
shared type, these definitions remain provisional here:

- **Explanatory progression:** ordered functional moves transforming an
  audience's starting representation into a target representation.
- **Proof obligation:** domain- and task-specific burden determining which
  warrants, alternatives, and uncertainty must be visible.
- **Writer-function model:** bounded architecture abstracted from a work,
  excluding distinctive persona and surface mimicry.
- **Authority laundering:** using the signals of institutional or expert
  authority without the process, evidence, or mandate that warrants them.
- **Compression amputation:** memorable simplification that removes a
  decision-critical boundary, uncertainty, or exception.

## Assumption-change rule

Increase confidence only after independent coding and outcome evidence. Reduce
confidence if a crossed corpus shows stronger author than proof-regime effects,
if architecture adds no predictive value beyond content/genre, or if declared
fit patterns systematically disadvantage an audience subgroup.

## Program-mandated future research seeds

document type is known; (2) audience expertise explains more variance than
proof obligation; (3) a small set of universal coherence operations dominates

in a crossed corpus.
