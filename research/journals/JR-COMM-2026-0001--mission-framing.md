# Research Journal Entry

## Objective

Translate the broad Communication Engineering mission into the first bounded, testable research project without falsely claiming exhaustive completion.

## Starting state

The repository was a greenfield ROS pilot. The charter excluded exhaustive taxonomy, named no users, and identified first-use-case selection as the largest uncertainty. No domain evidence, hypothesis, theory, or REP existed.

## Actions taken

Read the attached mission; root README; bootstrap; governance index, constitution, operating manual, REP specification; research, evidence, output, lifecycle, and supersession policies; current context; templates; schemas; and repository status. Compared candidate slices and inspected official standards, consensus synthesis, a controlled trial, a systematic review, and accessibility guidance.

## Observations

- The mission is a multi-project program, not a bounded REP.
- Existing governance requires a measurable first slice and prohibits unsupported discipline claims.
- Goal-audience-context alignment recurs across independent sources.
- Behavioral procedural tasks support stronger near-term validation than style catalogs.
- Plain language is promising but outcome- and context-dependent.

## Evidence collected

EV-COMM-2026-0001 through EV-COMM-2026-0006.

## Hypotheses considered

HY-COMM-2026-0001 and HY-COMM-2026-0002.

## Attempts to falsify

Searched for direct empirical plain-language effects and found both a large effect and a null-compatible effect within one trial. Considered high-stakes instructions but rejected them for the first pilot because safety and domain expertise would dominate feasibility.

## Decisions and rationale

Recommend a synthetic procedural-documentation experiment as the first slice. This is reversible and explicitly provisional pending user/stakeholder validation.

## Failures and dead ends

The requested exhaustive mission cannot be completed responsibly in one research cycle. No participant data or stakeholder interviews were available. The ISO full text was not publicly accessible.

## Confidence changes

Initial confidence in a universal style taxonomy as the right starting structure decreased. Confidence in conditional fit rose from low to medium; confidence that plain language is non-universal rose to high.

## Files changed

Created evidence, hypotheses, candidate theory, mission, partial REP, and updated compact context.

## Highest-value next step

Select one synthetic procedural task, preregister audience and outcome measures, then run a feasibility pilot before a powered comparison.
