# Research Journal

## 2026-08-02 — Scope

User requested coverage of literary greats, Beat poets, Fitzgerald, Hemingway,
Frost, and Asimov. Bounded “literary greats” to the named authors and three Beat
representatives. Declared works before analysis and excluded universal ranking.

## Evidence and comparison

Inspected stable primary editions for *Gatsby*, *The Sun Also Rises*, Frost's
poems, Ginsberg, Kerouac, and Ferlinghetti; used independent scholarship for
Asimov's audience adaptation and contextual sources for the Beats. Compared
function, reader role, information strategy, memory, and failure modes.

## Falsification

Searched for disconfirming variation within each cluster. Frost's poems resist
simple plainspoken certainty; Hemingway's work is not explained by sentence
length; Asimov varies by audience and genre; Ferlinghetti rejected a fixed Beat
label; Ginsberg's apparent spontaneity is structurally organized. These checks
reject author essence and movement uniformity.

## Stop decision

Stopped after the matrix and framework additions converged. Further close
readings would add examples without resolving causal effects, cultural scope,
or reception. Those require a larger crossed corpus and audience research.

## Next step

Test the inference-contract hypothesis on a low-risk narrative explanation,
then expand beyond this male, largely white U.S. canon.
