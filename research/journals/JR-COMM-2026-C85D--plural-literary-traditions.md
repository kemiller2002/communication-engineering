# Research Journal

## Scope and selection

Expanded all six requested areas with 29 unique writers. Used overlap rather
than exclusive demographic bins. Selected entry and deeper works that expose
different genres and developmental phases. Avoided claiming representativeness.

## Assumption challenges

Rejected: one Beat spontaneity; one Black aesthetic; one Indigenous
storytelling method; immigrant or queer as genres; English translation as
transparent; demographic inclusion alone as epistemic repair.

## Evidence and method

Reviewed movement collections, author biographies, hosted primary works,
interviews, bibliographies, and reference sources. Coded communication
functions and non-transferable boundaries. Did not reproduce copyrighted text.

## Stop decision

Stopped when each requested area had multiple internally contrasting writers,
a reading path, and explicit boundaries. Further expansion without original-
language expertise, community review, or a narrower hypothesis would add names
more than reliability.

## Next step

Community-reviewed Indigenous protocol study and translator-aware paired-text
analysis, followed by an experiment on contextualized adaptation versus persona
imitation.
