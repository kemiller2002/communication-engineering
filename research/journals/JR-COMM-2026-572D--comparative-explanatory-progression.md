# Research Journal

## 2026-08-02 — Repository reconstruction

Read root governance, both REP specifications, research/evidence policies,
current theory/state, program mission, bounded mission, templates, schemas, and
registries. Observed extensive pre-existing user changes and preserved them.
Resolved the broad prompt through approved program `MS-COMM-2026-0003` and
bounded mission `MS-COMM-2026-0004`.

## 2026-08-02 — Conflict and scope decision

The prompt requests one comprehensive cross-cultural writer system, while ROS
requires bounded missions and the current state forbids full corpus analysis
before freezing criteria and rubric. Chose the least-disruptive path: execute
the first five-domain method pilot and treat remaining breadth as program debt.
The prompt's “DF framework” conflicts with canonical `DF` meaning; created a
framework document plus `DF-COMM-2026-96CF` Decision Record.

## 2026-08-02 — Corpus and rubric

Defined explanatory progression and the unit, froze inclusion/exclusion
criteria, selected five stable primary works, and documented rejected
alternatives and cultural bias. Pilot coding showed that example and warrant
must be distinct and that navigation is a functional move in specifications;
froze rubric v1.0 after that single revision.

## 2026-08-02 — Evidence and analysis

Inspected primary texts for Darwin, Euclid, Brown, Descartes, and RFC 9110;
reviewed scholarship on competing Darwin argument models, Descartes's
practice-dependent method, and expository text-structure interventions.
Separated textual observation from inferred reader mechanism. Produced
profiles, matrix, contrasts, framework, patterns, anti-patterns, ethics gates,
and ten application cases.

## 2026-08-02 — Falsification and assumption challenges

- Famousness was excluded as evidence; RFC and Brown also challenge the
  individual-author premise.
- One stable writer style was rejected as an unsupported level of analysis.
- Short sentences, plain language, formality, emotion, memorability, and
  readability were not treated as universal outcome proxies.
- Cross-domain transfer was accepted only at the function level with explicit
  proof and context boundaries.
- Hybridization was rejected where audience roles or warrants conflict.
- Persuasion was constrained by truth, welfare, authority, accessibility, and
  uncertainty gates.

## 2026-08-02 — Repeated coding

Recoded opening, middle, and closing units for all five works after completing
the first-pass matrix. Primary-code agreement was 12/15; three cases became
dual-coded rather than forcing one label. This checks internal stability only.

## 2026-08-02 — Stop decision

Stopped at the bounded mission boundary. Additional desk examples were no
longer changing the taxonomy or fit model. The largest material uncertainties
require a second coder, non-Western corpus expansion, and comparative audience
experiments. The REP is complete for the pilot, not for the multi-REP program.
