# Research Journal Entry

## Objective

Review the complete portfolio, test existing priorities, normalize the queue as
a knowledge graph, and advance the highest-value dependency.

## Starting state

Five queue rows, one proposed mission, one partial REP, six evidence items, two
hypotheses, and one candidate theory. Queue entries lacked stable IDs, maturity,
relationships, confidence, exit evidence, and review triggers.

## Actions taken

Inspected all queue, mission, registry, current-state, risk, and pilot-plan
artifacts. Decomposed the program into twelve stable research questions. Scored
decision leverage, uncertainty reduction, dependency unlock, feasibility, and
risk reduction. Created a graph roadmap, stage gates, review cadence, and a
preliminary purpose-to-outcome matrix.

## Observations

The former priority order placed task selection before its measurement and
constraint dependencies. Outcome validity has greater immediate dependency
leverage than discipline-boundary research. AI strategy selection is
premature until context fields, effects, and safety boundaries are validated.

## Evidence collected

No new external evidence. This cycle synthesized repository evidence
EV-COMM-2026-0001 through 0006 and observed portfolio structure.

## Hypotheses considered

- A checklist queue is sufficient. Rejected for this portfolio because it
  cannot reconstruct dependencies or maturation.
- The procedural experiment can begin after choosing a task. Weakened:
  measurement and constraint gates must also be met.
- Outcome-measure research is secondary to task selection. Rejected for current
  sequencing because it unlocks candidate comparison and experiment validity.

## Attempts to falsify

Considered immediate database/schema automation. Rejected because two cycles
of manual use can test whether the richer representation earns its maintenance
cost. The roadmap explicitly schedules this falsification.

## Decisions and rationale

Accepted DF-COMM-2026-0001. Advanced RQ-COMM-003 to Background Research based
on the existing standards, trial, and systematic-review evidence. Kept
MS-COMM-2026-0001 proposed and added explicit dependency gates.

## Failures and dead ends

No user/stakeholder evidence was available, so RQ-COMM-001 could not advance.
No empirical validity review was completed for the preliminary measure matrix.

## Confidence changes

Confidence in the original ordinal priority list decreased. Confidence that
measurement work must precede experiment activation increased to High as a
portfolio sequencing judgment.

## Files changed

Created DF-COMM-2026-0001, this journal, and
`research/frontier/PORTFOLIO-ROADMAP.md`; replaced the compact queue view;
updated current state, mission dependencies, and risk ownership below.

## Highest-value next step

Complete RQ-COMM-003 scoping with explicit construct validity and metric
failure-mode criteria, while collecting user-need evidence for RQ-COMM-001.
