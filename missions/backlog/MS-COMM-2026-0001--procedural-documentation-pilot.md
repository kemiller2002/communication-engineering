# Mission

## Objective

Determine whether a structured communication intervention improves completion of a synthetic multi-step technical task for a specified audience.

## Why this matters

It creates the first behavioral test of TH-COMM-2026-0001 and a reusable measurement harness for later styles.

## Scope

### Included

One low-risk synthetic task; two controlled instruction variants; specified novice or intermediate audience; task success, critical errors, time, assistance, comprehension, and satisfaction.

### Excluded

Sensitive data, production changes, health/legal/safety instructions, persuasion, exhaustive taxonomy, and claims beyond the tested population and task.

## Existing context

RP-COMM-2026-0001, JR-COMM-2026-0001, TH-COMM-2026-0001.

## Initial hypotheses

HY-COMM-2026-0001 and HY-COMM-2026-0002.

## Required evidence

User/stakeholder need, task feasibility, preregistered protocol, participant results, failures, qualitative observations, and accessibility review.

## Constraints

Use synthetic fixtures. Separate wording, structure, and visual changes where feasible. Do not infer comprehension from preference.

Do not activate this mission until RQ-COMM-001 has a named audience and task,
RQ-COMM-003 has selected primary and guardrail measures, and RQ-COMM-004 has
completed the task-specific data, accessibility, and harm review.

## Execution instructions

Choose a task with neither floor nor ceiling effects; pilot it; freeze variants and scoring; then compare with representative participants. Preserve anonymized aggregate data and analysis.

## Deliverables

Experiment record, evidence records, journal, REP, theory update, and reusable evaluation rubric.

## Success criteria

The study estimates differences on preregistered outcomes, reports uncertainty and failures, and determines whether the method is sensitive enough for another cycle.

## Stop conditions

Stop if realistic execution requires sensitive data, task effects are dominated by environment, or feasibility testing shows irreducible floor/ceiling effects.

## Handoff requirements

Record recruitment criteria, exact variants, task environment, scoring rules, raw anonymized results, commands, limitations, and next discriminating test.
