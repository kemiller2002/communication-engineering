# Mission

Compare bounded works by F. Scott Fitzgerald, Ernest Hemingway, Robert Frost,
Isaac Asimov, and representative Beat writers Allen Ginsberg, Jack Kerouac,
and Lawrence Ferlinghetti. Identify functional techniques, transfer conditions,
and failure modes without ranking authors or treating a movement as uniform.

## Corpus

- Fitzgerald: *The Great Gatsby*, especially chapters 1, 5, 7, and 9.
- Hemingway: *The Sun Also Rises* and his omission principle as stated in
  *Death in the Afternoon*.
- Frost: “Mending Wall” and “After Apple-Picking.”
- Asimov: *Foundation* as idea-driven fiction and “The Relativity of Wrong” as
  popular scientific argument; conclusions about his full output are excluded.
- Beats: Ginsberg's “Howl,” Kerouac's “Essentials of Spontaneous Prose,” and
  Ferlinghetti's “Dog” plus contextual material on *A Coney Island of the Mind*.

## Success and stop conditions

Success requires primary-text-grounded profiles, direct comparisons, explicit
anti-transfer rules, and evidence-linked integration with the fit framework.
Stop at desk-analysis conclusions; causal audience effects require experiments.
