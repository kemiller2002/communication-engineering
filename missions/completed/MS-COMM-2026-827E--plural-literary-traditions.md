# Mission

Correct the initial literary pilot's predominantly white male U.S. canon by
adding women Beats, Harlem Renaissance and Black Arts writers, distinct
Indigenous traditions, immigrant and queer writers, and non-U.S. traditions.
Produce bounded functional comparisons and a works-to-read guide.

## Boundary

This is a diversity expansion, not a representative world canon. Identity is
context, not a stylistic code. Indigenous writers are identified by their
specific nations where source documentation supports it, and overlapping
writers are not forced into one category.
